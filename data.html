Informed Consent Form

HCI2 – Usability Evaluation · Group 4

You are warmly invited to take part in this study. Its primary goal is to evaluate the usability of the Daily Self-Care Planner Application, assessing how effectively, efficiently, and satisfactorily users can interact with it. The findings will provide valuable insights to identify usability issues and improve the system's design and overall user experience. The Daily Self-Care Planner Application is designed to help users manage daily tasks, track habits, and monitor their mood for better self-care.

If you choose to participate, you will use the application prototype, explore its features such as task management, habit tracking, and mood monitoring, answer a short System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaire, and provide brief feedback about your experience. This activity will take approximately 10–15 minutes, depending on your pace.

Your participation is completely voluntary. You are free to participate or not, and you may withdraw at any time without penalty. Your responses will be treated with confidentiality. Your identity will remain private, and all collected data will be used strictly for academic purposes only.

By proceeding with the evaluation, you confirm that you have read and understood this consent form and agree to participate.

📐 Sample Size – Slovin's Formula

n = N ÷ (1 + Ne²)
n = 43 ÷ (1 + 43 × 0.05²)
n = 43 ÷ (1 + 0.1075)
n = 43 ÷ 1.1075
n ≈ 40 respondents

Where N = 43 (total population), e = 0.05 (5% margin of error)

Each group member independently evaluated the prototype using Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics. Severity rated 0–4 per heuristic, averaged across all 5 evaluators.

H1

Visibility of System Status Low · Avg 0.6

System keeps users informed through appropriate feedback.

✅ Progress rings, streak badges, and checkmarks clearly show status at all times.
H2

Match Between System and Real World Low · Avg 0.2

Use words and concepts familiar to the user.

✅ Labels use everyday language — "Drink Water", "Workout", "Bed time". Emoji icons reinforce meaning.
H3

User Control and Freedom Medium · Avg 2.2

Clearly marked emergency exits for mistaken actions.

⚠️ Skip and Cancel buttons exist but no undo for deleted habits — a gap found by 3 evaluators.
H4

Consistency and Standards Low · Avg 0.0

Same words, situations, and actions mean the same thing.

✅ Green header, white cards, and bottom nav are consistent across all 8 screens.
H5

Error Prevention Medium · Avg 1.8

Careful design prevents problems from occurring.

⚠️ Placeholder hints help, but no confirmation dialog before deleting tasks or habits.
H6

Recognition Rather Than Recall Low · Avg 0.2

Minimize memory load by making options visible.

✅ All icons paired with text labels. No icon-only elements found throughout the prototype.
H7

Flexibility and Efficiency of Use Medium · Avg 2.0

Accelerators for expert users.

⚠️ Quick Actions row helps. However, no swipe gestures available for power users.
H8

Aesthetic and Minimalist Design Low · Avg 0.0

No irrelevant or rarely needed information.

✅ Each screen focuses on one task. Clean card layouts with ample whitespace.
H9

Help Users Recognize and Recover from Errors High · Avg 3.6

Error messages in plain language that suggest solutions.

🔴 No error states or validation messages designed for empty forms. Most cited issue by all 5 evaluators.
H10

Help and Documentation High · Avg 3.4

Help may be necessary even for well-designed systems.

🔴 No onboarding tutorial or tooltip guidance. Intensity slider and streak dots confuse new users.
Overall Heuristic Severity Average 1.4 / 4.0 — Minor but Notable Issues

System Usability Scale (SUS) Instrument

40 respondents answered using a 5-point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree).

Q1

I think that I would use this system frequently.

Positive item — contribution = score − 1

Q2

I found the system unnecessarily complex.

Negative item — contribution = 5 − score

Q3

I thought the system was easy to use.

Positive item — contribution = score − 1

Q4

I think I would need technical support to use this system.

Negative item — contribution = 5 − score

Q5

I found the various functions in this system were well integrated.

Positive item — contribution = score − 1

Q6

I thought there was too much inconsistency in this system.

Negative item — contribution = 5 − score

Q7

I would imagine that most people would learn to use this system very quickly.

Positive item — contribution = score − 1

Q8

I found the system very cumbersome to use.

Negative item — contribution = 5 − score

Q9

I felt very confident using this system.

Positive item — contribution = score − 1

Q10

I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this system.

Negative item — contribution = 5 − score
