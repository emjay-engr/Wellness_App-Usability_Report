📐 SUS Formula Applied

1
Positive questions (Q1,3,5,7,9): Contribution = Scale position − 1
e.g. Q3 mean 4.20 → 4.20 − 1 = 3.20
2
Negative questions (Q2,4,6,8,10): Contribution = 5 − Scale position
e.g. Q8 mean 2.55 → 5 − 2.55 = 2.45
3
Sum all contributions: 2.98+1.83+3.20+2.02+2.92+2.33+2.80+2.45+2.85+1.73 = 25.11
4
Multiply by 2.5: 25.11 × 2.5 = 62.78

🔍 Findings

Key Findings & Discussion

⭐

Strongest Area: Q3 – Ease of Use

Q3 ("I thought the system was easy to use") had the highest SUS contribution of 3.20 / 4.00. 87.5% of respondents agreed or strongly agreed — the clearest strength of the Wellness Super App.

💪

Strong Area: Confidence & Frequency

Q1 (use frequently, 2.98) and Q9 (felt confident, 2.85) both scored well. Most respondents see themselves using the app regularly and feel comfortable navigating it.

⚠️

Weak Area: Q10 – Learning Curve

Q10 ("needed to learn a lot before getting going") had the lowest contribution at 1.73 / 4.00. 47.5% of respondents agreed they needed prior learning — pointing to the missing onboarding flow.

🔧

Weak Area: Q2 – Perceived Complexity

Q2 ("unnecessarily complex") scored 1.83. 37.5% of respondents agreed with complexity statements — aligned with the heuristic finding that H9 (error recovery) and H10 (help/docs) are missing.

📊

SUS Score Interpretation

A score of 62.78 falls in the "Marginal / OK" range on the SUS adjective scale — above the failing threshold of 51, below the acceptable 68 industry baseline. The app is functional but needs improvements before full deployment.

🎯

Gap from Industry Baseline

The industry average SUS score is 68. The app scores 5.22 points below baseline. Addressing the learning curve (Q10) and complexity (Q2) issues would be the fastest path to reaching and exceeding 68.

✅ Recommendations

Design Improvement Recommendations

Prioritized based on SUS question scores — highest impact items first.

Priority 1
📖

Add Onboarding Tutorial

Q10 scored lowest (1.73). A 3-step onboarding flow on first launch will reduce the learning curve and directly improve the SUS score on this question.

+Q10 impact
Priority 2
🛡️

Add Form Validation

Q2 complexity score (1.83) can be improved with clear error messages and inline validation — reducing the perception of complexity when things go wrong.

+Q2 impact
Priority 3
↩️

Undo / Confirm Delete

Q4 (technical support needed, 2.02) can improve if users feel safer interacting — an undo toast removes fear of mistakes without needing help from others.

+Q4 impact
Priority 4
📭

Design Empty States

Blank screens when no data exists confuse users (contributes to Q10 learning curve). Clear empty state messages with CTAs guide users naturally forward.

Priority 5
👆

Add Swipe Gestures

Adding common swipe interactions (mark done, dismiss) improves Q8 (cumbersome, 2.45) and Q7 (easy to learn, 2.80) — familiar gestures reduce friction.
